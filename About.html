Our Story

Since 1995

A House Built on Passion

Savoria began as a single room with twelve seats and an uncompromising belief: that a meal could be a memory that lasts a lifetime.



Three decades later, that room has grown into one of Europe's most celebrated dining destinations — yet the belief has never changed. Every morning, our chefs walk the markets before sunrise. Every evening, our team welcomes guests as if they were the only ones in the world. We have earned three Michelin stars, but our proudest measure remains the same: the guests who return, year after year, to celebrate the moments that matter most.
